# BA T12 – HR Attrition Analysis Dashboard

## Objective
Analyze the assigned HR Analytics dataset in Tableau Public to identify employee attrition patterns and suggest ways to improve employee retention.

## Dataset
**HR Analytics** — 1,470 employee records.

## Dashboard Visualizations
1. **Overall Attrition (Pie Chart):** Employees who stayed vs. employees who left.
2. **Attrition by Department (Bar Chart):** Attrition rates across departments.
3. **Attrition by Age Group (Line Chart):** Attrition rates for different age groups.
4. **Department × Business Travel (Heat Map):** Attrition rates by department and travel frequency.
5. **Attrition by Education Field (Treemap):** Employee distribution and attrition by education field.

## Interactive Filters
- Department
- Business Travel
- Education Field

## Key Findings
1. **Overall attrition:** 237 out of 1,470 employees left (**16.12%**).
2. **Age:** Employees aged 18–24 have the highest age-group attrition rate (**39.18%**).
3. **Department:** Sales has the highest departmental attrition rate (**20.63%**).
4. **Business travel:** Frequent travelers have **24.91%** attrition, compared with **8.00%** for non-travel employees.

## Suggestions and Recommendations
1. Improve onboarding, mentoring, and career-development support for younger employees.
2. Review workload and travel requirements, especially for Sales employees and frequent travelers.

## Overall Conclusion
Employee attrition is higher among younger employees, Sales employees, and frequent business travelers. Targeted retention efforts may help address these patterns. The data shows associations, not the reasons employees left.
